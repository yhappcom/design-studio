## UX integration failure conditions
The integrated workflow fails if any domain causes:
- record identity loss;
- certainty or safe-action mutation caused by viewport/theme/locale rather than state;
- outcome-unknown blind retry;
- recovery without object/task/certainty restoration;
- state meaning dependent on color alone;
- required qualifier deletion for fit;
- operational literal corruption under RTL/localization;
- visual reordering that contradicts source/focus/task order.
