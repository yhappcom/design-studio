## CONTRADICTION REVIEW
Candidate 05's warm ivory/mint atmosphere and Candidate 07's sage-neutral/ledger atmosphere are legitimate alternatives. A studio rule prescribing one surface temperature, one accent proportion or one exact palette would contradict the evidence that both can carry the same product semantics.

The transferable invariant is **semantic rank and redundancy**, not palette sameness:
1. focus, current location, selection, invalid/error, pending, offline/degraded, recovery and evidenced success remain distinguishable states;
2. brand accent does not automatically own focus, selection or success;
3. consequence-bearing state outranks decorative brand restraint when conflict occurs;
4. meaning survives non-color cues and supported accessibility modes;
5. success color appears only after Interaction authority establishes success;
6. native high-contrast and Flutter Web forced-colors remain separate evidence classes.

Concept variables include surface hue/temperature, accent hue, decorative tint, exact chroma/lightness distribution and atmosphere-specific contrast rhythm, provided semantic rank and required contrast survive.

## PRACTICE / CRITIQUE
Reject two opposite failure modes: (a) normalizing Candidate 07 to Candidate 05 mint/ivory to claim system consistency; (b) allowing each concept to redefine error/focus/success semantics for aesthetic purity. Both confuse style with state truth.

Static light/dark renders cannot validate injected runtime states, forced colors, native high contrast, glare/night or observer performance.
