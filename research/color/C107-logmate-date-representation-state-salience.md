# C107 — LogMate Date Representation State-Salience Transfer

Status: **STAGE 3 PRACTICE / TRANSFER VALIDATION — no gate promotion**

## PURPOSE
Ensure H3 date-width adaptation cannot collapse semantic Color states.

## RELATED DOMAIN CHECK
T076, I094/L098, W107 and CD113 checked. This extends C106 from large-text representation change to date-format/width change.

## PRACTICE
Across compact/expanded/native date realizations preserve independent channels for focus, selection, invalidity, ambiguity, recovery and committed state. A date becoming wider, wrapped, disclosed or browser-native does not authorize stronger decorative chroma or weaker functional state cues.

## CRITIQUE
A compact date chip can accidentally become both a date container and a semantic-state badge. This couples format/width to meaning and fails under forced colors or representation transfer.

## SYNTHESIS
Date representation is not a semantic Color state. SC-C quiet functional boundary applies only where state/boundary meaning exists; formatting variation itself stays neutral.

## OPEN
Rendered light/night/forced-colors production evidence; stale-state cleanup after date edit; calibrated display/glare/human evidence.

## HANDOFFS TO OTHER SPECIALISTS
- Layout/Content: do not use color to compensate for ambiguous date ordering.
- Web: capture used-state/focus behavior for native and authored date controls separately.
- Type: Color does not justify metric compression.
