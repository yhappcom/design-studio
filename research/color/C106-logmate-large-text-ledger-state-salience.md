# C106 — LogMate Large-Text Ledger State-Salience Transfer

Status: **STAGE 3 PRACTICE / TRANSFER VALIDATION — no gate promotion**

## PURPOSE
Resolve the Color side of H2: when a dense ledger enlarges, wraps, scrolls or discloses secondary information, semantic state must remain legible without turning color into the sole carrier or increasing decorative competition.

## RELATED DOMAIN CHECK
- Type T075/T074: mature-role metrics and no typographic compression rescue.
- Layout/Interaction I092/L096: semantic axes, focus/recovery and conditional boundaries.
- Web W105: runtime/forced-colors/independent-engine evidence required.
- Content CD111: state/consequence/recovery language remains protected.
- WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.10, 1.4.12, 1.4.11 and 2.4.11 treated as separate acceptance axes.

## PRACTICE MATRIX
Test the same row under baseline and enlarged/reflowed representations for:
- rest;
- keyboard focus;
- pointer/touch selection;
- invalid/error;
- ambiguous/import-review;
- pending;
- recovery/Undo available;
- committed result;
- stale/expired recovery;
- forced-colors.

For each state, verify that geometry/content provides a non-color distinction and that color reinforces rather than defines the state.

## CRITIQUE
Large text creates a predictable failure pressure: compact separators disappear, wrapping increases visual mass, and designers may overuse accent fills to restore scanability. That is rejected. SC-C Quiet Functional Boundary requires decorative competition to reduce before functional salience. A focused or invalid row may become more explicit; the entire enlarged ledger should not become more chromatic merely because density changed.

If horizontal scrolling is retained for the table, sticky/frozen regions must not hide the focused component. WCAG 2.2 SC 2.4.11 is a distinct interaction/layout requirement; Color cannot claim compliance by making an obscured focus state brighter.

## SYNTHESIS
The invariant is **semantic salience rank**, not identical RGB or identical surface area. Enlarged/reflowed ledger representations may change boundary area and grouping, but focus, invalidity, ambiguity and recovery must remain distinguishable from brand/decorative chroma and from each other.

## OPEN
- No production palette.
- No rendered light/night/forced-colors large-text evidence.
- No independent-engine/device/glare/observer/human evidence.

## HANDOFFS TO OTHER SPECIALISTS
- Layout/Web: state geometry may expand with text; do not compensate by flooding rows with accent color.
- Content: state words remain necessary where color loss would erase meaning.
- Type: color cannot rescue cramped or clipped operational strings.
