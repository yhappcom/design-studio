## SYNTHESIS
### IP-C1 — Brand color is not semantic authority
Brand/accent, focus, selection, invalid/ambiguous, warning/recovery, success and persistence must remain distinct roles. Never reuse one signature accent as the only carrier of materially different states.

### IP-C2 — Restraint removes decorative competition before functional salience
A calm visual system may reduce persistent decorative chroma, but not focus/error/recovery salience needed to understand or operate the product.

### IP-C3 — Light/night consistency is relational, not literal
Preserve role hierarchy and semantic distinction across appearances; literal color values may change to maintain contrast and perceptual balance.

### IP-C4 — State meaning survives authored-color loss
Required distinctions must remain operable under forced colors/user overrides through structure, labels, focus geometry and other non-color cues.

### IP-C5 — Environmental claims require environmental evidence
A dark screenshot is not night/glare validation. Do not promote cockpit/night-readability or calibrated-color claims without physical-device/environment evidence.

## PRACTICE / CRITIQUE
Stress-applied IP-C1..C5 to Home metrics, ledger rows, Activity filters, Add Flight validation and import ambiguity/recovery. A sparse brand system is viable only if semantic states retain independent tokens and non-color cues. No hue family is required by these principles.

## REPRODUCIBLE VALIDATION CONTRACT
For each candidate system record semantic token role, light/night value, contrast result where applicable, focus/selection/error/recovery differentiation, forced-colors behavior and stale-state cleanup. Execute on the same cross-surface fixture and repeat in an independent engine before promotion.
