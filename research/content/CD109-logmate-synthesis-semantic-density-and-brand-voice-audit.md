## Semantic precedence
For LogMate content, the proposed synthesis order is:
1. professional meaning and safety-relevant distinction;
2. actual state/action/consequence/recovery truth;
3. stable terminology and object identity;
4. localization-ready semantic structure;
5. concise scan-friendly realization;
6. tone/brand character.

Tone cannot rewrite the state model.

## Contradiction matrix
- **Conciseness vs professional semantics — Class D:** abbreviate only where the abbreviation is established, unambiguous in context and governed; otherwise solve geometry elsewhere.
- **Calm voice vs consequential warning — Class D:** remain concise and non-alarmist, but do not soften destructive/ambiguous/recovery consequences until they become vague.
- **Brand consistency vs surface adaptation — Class B:** preserve terminology and voice principles, not identical sentence length across Home, forms, ledger, status and accessibility payloads.
- **Localization vs literal source evidence — Class A if translation overwrites source:** preserve imported/source labels and identifiers as evidence; localize explanatory interface around them.
- **Minimal UI vs discoverability — Class D:** omit explanatory text only when label, state and recovery remain clear through other non-color, accessible channels.

## Candidate brand-voice properties
PROJECT-SPECIFIC CANDIDATE: precise, quiet, operational, non-theatrical, consequence-explicit, recovery-forward. Avoid luxury adjectives, cockpit cosplay, motivational filler and technical bravado. This is a voice corridor candidate, not production copy approval.

## Complete-system stress corpus
Future synthesis validation should include:
- Home summary/action labels;
- Add Flight field labels/helper/error/recovery;
- View Logbook compact headers and empty/filter/search states;

For each string record semantic contract, locale, surface, visible/a11y realization, wrap/truncation, action consequence, recovery availability and whether shortening changed meaning.

## CRITIQUE
A brand philosophy built around reduction can easily become content under-specification. The defensible interpretation is not “fewer words at all costs” but “no words without a job, and no required semantic job left undone.” Human comprehension and pilot terminology preference remain OPEN.
