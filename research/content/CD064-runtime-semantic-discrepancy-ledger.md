## LEDGER SCHEMA
Each discrepancy records:
- build/scenario/runtime identity;
- semantic object and measure;
- scope/time/basis;
- polarity;
- certainty/finality;
- availability/integrity;
- source string/message ID;
- locale realization;
- visible realization;
- accessible realization;
- history/export realization when applicable;
- observed discrepancy;
- owning domain;
- proposed revision;
- regression scenarios.

## NON-NEGOTIABLE DISTINCTIONS
Do not normalize:
- distribution into profit;
- partial/unavailable into zero;
- estimated into final;
- gross into net;
- tax refund into generic income;
- additional tax into generic loss;
- ambiguous outcome into success/failure.

If complete wording creates fit pressure, log Type/Layout dependency instead of deleting truth. If the underlying state/action/recovery is ambiguous, log Interaction dependency instead of polishing around it.
