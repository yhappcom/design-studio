## Complete system
The content model now spans five product moments rather than isolated messages:

1. onboarding/preparation — explain what the operation does, prerequisites and consequence before commitment;
2. form/input — stable labels, format help, validation tied to the field and corrective action;
3. pending/state — describe what is happening without asserting success/failure prematurely;
4. recovery/retrieval — distinguish known failure from outcome unknown; preserve `Check outcome` before unsafe retry; make prior/local records findable when relevant;
5. confirmation/history — state what changed, identify the resulting record/object and provide a route back to it.
