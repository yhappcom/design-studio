# CD111 — LogMate Signature-Code Content Transfer Audit

Status: **STAGE 3 PRACTICE / CONTRADICTION REVIEW — no voice approval**

## PURPOSE
Verify that the completed signature stack can transfer without turning visual economy into semantic deletion.

## RELATED DOMAIN CHECK
T074, C105, I092/L096, W104 and coordinator signature/geometry contracts checked. CD096–CD110 state/import/content-system contracts remain authoritative where applicable.

## CONTENT INVARIANT
Order of authority:
professional meaning → actual object/state/action/consequence/recovery → stable terminology → accessible payload → concise realization → candidate voice/brand character.

SC-A/SC-B may change presentation hierarchy but cannot rename or collapse professional concepts. SC-C may reduce decorative chrome but cannot remove required labels, instructions, validation or recovery. SC-D may make recovery feel direct but cannot say Undo when the inverse is unavailable, expired or failed.

## CROSS-SURFACE CONTENT SET
- Home: Search, Current Period, Recent, Activity, Totals and bottom-nav labels remain semantically distinct.
- Activity: 7 / 28 / 90 / Custom are current canonical period realizations; repeated `Last` is not required.
- Add Flight: field labels/instructions/validation remain explicit enough to preserve professional meaning.
- View Logbook: canonical headers and totals terminology are protected from aesthetic abbreviation.
- Customize/import: mapping provenance, ambiguity, exclusion, commit and recovery remain different states.

## LANGUAGE SCOPE
LogMate product-authored UI is English-only at present. Unicode/source/user content, Remark, crew/source text and numeric date locale ordering are separate stress dimensions; they do not create a multilingual product-copy requirement.

## CANDIDATE VOICE CHECK
`precise / quiet / operational / non-theatrical` remains a useful candidate only when it does not cause:
- false certainty;
- hidden consequence;
- collapsed pending/failure/ambiguous states;
- vague generic actions such as OK where object/consequence matters;
- premature Saved/Synced claims.

## FAILURE CONDITIONS
- protected wording shortened solely for geometry;
- color/motion referenced as the only state explanation;
- browser lifecycle language presented as persistence truth;
- exact/possible duplicate or ambiguous/invalid collapsed for brevity;
- source labels silently normalized into misleading product terminology.

## SYNTHESIS
The content expression of LogMate's signature is **semantic economy**, not minimum word count: no redundant language, but every required semantic job remains present.

## OPEN
No production voice approval, linguistic review, AT comprehension or representative-pilot comprehension/task evidence exists.
