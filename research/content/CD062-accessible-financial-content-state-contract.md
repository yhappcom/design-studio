# CD062 — Accessible Financial Content-State Contract

Status: PRODUCT SYSTEM TRANSFER / PRACTICE
Date: 2026-09-17

## Question
How does CD061 survive localization, accessibility scaling and nonvisual/state presentation without semantic loss?

## PRACTICE
Every material financial output carries enough language to recover object, measure, scope, polarity, certainty/finality, availability and basis where needed. Labels and accessible names must not rely on nearby color alone. Estimated, final, partial and unavailable are state terms, not synonyms for positive/negative. Errors state what happened, what remains preserved, and the next corrective/recovery action.

## STRESS CORPUS
KRW/USD; positive/negative/zero; large signed amounts; gross/net; estimated/final ROC; partial/unavailable market data; tax adjustment; long Korean/English labels; export/history contexts. Shortening may remove repetition but not a truth-bearing qualifier.

## CRITIQUE
Content fit is not a valid reason to collapse distinct concepts such as distributions and total return, or estimated and final ROC. If a complete truthful string does not fit, T025/L047 must absorb the pressure through typography/layout changes.

## REPRODUCIBLE VALIDATION
Bind every string capture to W056 build/scenario identity. Compare visible label, accessible name, state announcement, error/recovery copy and export/history wording. Maintain a discrepancy ledger for implementation drift. Actual ARB/TMS round-trip, linguistic review, AT comprehension and representative-human task evidence remain OPEN.
