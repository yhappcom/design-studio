## 3. Decisive capstone evidence

CD017 fixes one generic professional-record problem and keeps its state/action constraints constant across three complete content systems:
- A Task-Direct / Contextual Assistance;
- B Guided / Explanation-First;
- C Expert-Dense / Command-Oriented.

The alternatives differ in information timing, instructional depth, state expression, action naming, compactness, novice/expert handling and localization burden; they are not cosmetic rewrites.

A is selected conditionally using explicit criteria because it preserves semantic fidelity, primary-task directness, point-of-need explanation, recovery semantics and localization portability without inventing prerequisites. B is rejected for the fixed substrate because it front-loads unsupported education; C is rejected as a complete system because compression collapses material state/action distinctions.

The capstone also preserves an arithmetic correction in its weighted comparison rather than hiding the error. The score is explicitly treated as studio-judgment consistency support, not empirical user evidence.

## 5. What Stage 2 PASS means

Content Design can now, at intermediate professional-practice level:
- model user-facing language from product truth rather than prose preference;
- design forms and repair content;
- distinguish lifecycle/uncertainty/recovery states;
- time onboarding/help relative to decisions;
- distinguish search/filter/sort/settings semantics;
- modulate tone without sacrificing truth;
- construct localization-ready semantic contracts rather than English-string logic;
- compare complete content strategies and defend a conditional direction;
- integrate peer evidence without crossing ownership boundaries;
- identify where implementation/human evidence is still required.

---

## 6. What Stage 2 PASS does NOT mean

It does not establish:
- human comprehension, trust, preference, workload or task-performance improvement;
- production brand voice acceptance;
- actual LogMate/MintTap terminology correctness;
- real translator/localization workflow;
- message-format implementation;
- RTL/bidi correctness;
- screen-reader/AT behavior;
- native/browser parity;
- notification/email/cross-channel system completeness;
- product-wide terminology governance;
- enterprise string ownership/change propagation;
- production analytics/telemetry effects;
- Stage 3, 4 or 5 completion.

These are preserved for later stages/project validation rather than fabricated as closure evidence.
