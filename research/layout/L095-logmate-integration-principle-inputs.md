## SYNTHESIS
### IP-L1 — Stable geometry means stable semantic relationships
Preserve local comparison axes, semantic column boundaries, reading/action order and focus/recovery relationships. Do not freeze whole-screen coordinates across surfaces or breakpoints.

### IP-L2 — Density is governed, not maximized or minimized
Professional density is acceptable when grouping, hierarchy, alignment and target geometry remain legible. Whitespace is structural; sparse appearance is not itself premium.

### IP-L3 — Recomposition precedes semantic damage
At narrow/enlarged conditions, recompose, wrap, expand or use controlled 2D scrolling where meaning requires it before shrinking text or deleting protected semantics.

### IP-L4 — Cross-surface identity transfers as rules
Home, View Logbook, Activity, Add Flight and configuration/import surfaces may have different compositions. Identity promotion requires the same governing relationships to survive all relevant surfaces.

### IP-L5 — Functional boundaries survive low chrome
Alignment/whitespace may replace boxes and dividers only where grouping, affordance, focus, target and state remain unambiguous.
