Status: **STAGE 3 PRACTICE / CONTRADICTION REVIEW — no geometry gate promotion**

## PURPOSE
Turn the completed pre-concept Operational Geometry Contract into a falsifiable cross-surface audit for the new white-canvas concept phase.

## RELATED DOMAIN CHECK

## CROSS-SURFACE INVARIANTS
### Home
Repeated Date/Flight/Route/duration relationships may move as a region, but row-to-row comparison axes must remain stable. Bottom navigation must not obscure scroll content and must remain adaptable while NAV-001 is open.

### View Logbook
Preserve canonical semantic columns, shared header/body/totals offset, controlled horizontal scrolling when semantic width exceeds viewport, and sticky header ownership. Enlarged text may recompose; 36px normal rhythm is not a 200% contract.

### Activity
7/28/90/Custom remains one control family. Selection cannot depend on color alone. Repeated Flight rows inherit operational treatment.

### Add Flight
Forms reflow; they do not inherit ledger horizontal-scroll exceptions. Validation/recovery stays local and visible under low chrome.

### Customize / Import
Source→target→state/action and reorder candidate/recovery relationships must remain explicit; dense professional information is not a reason to collapse provenance.

## ADVERSARIAL TEST OF SC-A
Calibrated Axis fails if designers convert a semantic relationship into a decorative global rail. The axis may be invisible. Cross-surface transfer means equivalent comparison logic, not matching x-coordinates.

## ADVERSARIAL TEST OF SC-C
Quiet Functional Boundary fails if reduced chrome removes grouping, target ownership, focus position, ambiguity or recovery. 'Quiet' is subordinate to functional boundary need.

## STRESS LEDGER
Every concept should be checked with wide/narrow Flight IDs, locale-ordered numeric dates, long registrations, DEP/ARR combinations, H+MM and 99,999+59, long Unicode Remark/source text, canonical English warnings/recovery, baseline/enlarged text/text spacing, focus/selected/invalid/ambiguous/recovery.

## FAILURE CONDITIONS
- global coordinate freezing;
- whole-string Flight centering;
- per-character fixed cells;
- crushing ledger columns to avoid legitimate horizontal scroll;
- forcing Remark into operational mono;
- bottom dock obscuration;
- shortening protected wording for fit;
- claiming responsive/200% success from a static mockup.

## SYNTHESIS
The signature stack is geometrically coherent only if SC-A is interpreted as **local semantic calibration** and SC-C as **conditional boundary expression**. This creates a strong rejection filter for concept work without choosing exact aesthetics.

## OPEN
