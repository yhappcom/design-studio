- **Type:** use mature fallback; do not freeze provisional T021 metrics.

## Spatial hierarchy
Preserve this relationship where relevant:
`batch/context → graph validity + policy/revision → affected dependency region → prerequisite/member identity + outcome → reason → safe action/recheck → provenance/history`.

Graph-level invalidity must not be visually attached to only the nearest row. Member-level denial/failure must not visually read as the reason the whole graph is invalid unless that is authoritative truth.

## Stress conditions
Validate wide/narrow layouts, 200% text, long localized labels, filtering, collapsed groups, selection/focus changes, sorted lists, deep links and print/export. If a graphical edge view cannot survive narrow/zoom/static transfer, fall back to explicit structured prerequisite labels rather than preserving decorative connector geometry at the cost of meaning.

## Failure conditions
FAIL if reflow changes apparent dependency direction; sorting/filtering makes row adjacency look causal; a hidden prerequisite leaves a dependent with no discoverable reason; graph-level status scrolls/disappears while actionable members remain; or print/export loses the dependency reason/provenance needed to interpret blocked members.
