## PURPOSE
Separate protected spatial relationships from candidate-specific composition by comparing Candidate 05's continuous instrument-like sheet with Candidate 07's rule-based ledger.

## CONTRADICTION REVIEW
The candidates intentionally use different grouping devices, density and visual mass. Therefore card count, divider count, exact gutters, section spacing, row height and 390×844 coordinates are not studio invariants.

The reusable protected relationships are:
1. pilot/page identity ↔ Settings/control ownership;
2. primary action ↔ destination/action meaning;
3. period control ↔ selected period ↔ values it governs;
4. flight identity ↔ route/date/time/duration values belonging to that record;
5. Activity range ↔ committed selection ↔ projected statistics;
6. focus/state/recovery cue ↔ the object or control it describes;
7. reading/focus order ↔ semantic order after adaptive recomposition.

Candidate-specific variables include containment style, dividers vs continuous surfaces, exact whitespace rhythm, editorial vs instrument density, local alignment strategy and whether secondary information moves/wraps under stress.

## PRACTICE / CRITIQUE
Two invalid transfer strategies are identified. First, adding cards to Candidate 07 during stress merely because Candidate 05 uses stronger containment; second, flattening Candidate 05 into ledger rules merely to standardize geometry. Adaptive repair should preserve each concept's own composition until semantic relationships require a change.

## REPRODUCIBLE VALIDATION
For both exact runtimes capture baseline, narrow width, representative SafeArea, maximum supported text scaling and mature fallback. Record bounding boxes, line/row growth, reading/focus order, target geometry, clipping/scroll behavior and relationship ownership. A difference between candidates is acceptable unless one of the seven protected relationships fails.

## RESULT
The comparison supports **relationship invariance with composition diversity**. It narrows what Design Studio should standardize and prevents candidate convergence masquerading as systems maturity. No Stage 3 PASS.

## OPEN
Actual runtime geometry, physical-device SafeArea, focus/target measurement, AT and representative-human task evidence.
