# 4. Why later gaps do not invalidate Stage 2

The Master Curriculum places the following at Stage 3 or Stage 4, not Stage 2 closure:
- one coherent system across at least three form factors;
- motion/reduced-motion system;
- navigation/state systems across multiple form factors;
- production framework/device comparison;
- reproducible production prototypes and discrepancy tracking;
- keyboard/touch/pointer production validation;
- assistive-technology evidence;
- end-to-end production handoff.

Human task-performance, recognition, workload and preference are also explicitly deferred to app-development validation by user instruction. They are not simulated or counted as PASS evidence.

Stage 2 PASS therefore means intermediate professional product-design judgment is evidenced. It does **not** mean production or human validation is complete.
