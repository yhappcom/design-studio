# Layout & Spatial Design Research

This directory contains source-grounded research owned primarily by the **Layout & Spatial Design Specialist**.

## Scope

Research belongs here when its primary question concerns spatial organization, including:

- perceptual grouping and figure-ground organization;
- grid, alignment, columns, modules, and baselines;
- proportion, scale, visual mass, balance, and optical centering;
- whitespace, spacing relationships, density, and rhythm;
- hierarchy expressed through spatial relationships;
- responsive/adaptive recomposition;
- containment, regions, boundaries, and spatial ownership;
- cross-surface spatial systems.

Typography, color, accessibility, and interaction may appear when they materially affect spatial behavior, but research whose primary subject belongs to another discipline should remain in that discipline's research area.

## Numbering

Study numbers remain part of the studio-wide research chronology. Moving a study into this directory does **not** renumber it. This preserves references between research, exercises, critiques, and `progress/STATUS.md`.

## Evidence rule

The same Design Studio evidence model applies here:

- **SOURCE** — what an authoritative reference explicitly establishes;
- **SYNTHESIS** — a transferable principle inferred from evidence;
- **STUDIO JUDGMENT** — a design position or reusable method;
- **OPEN** — unresolved questions requiring further study or validation.

No Layout topic reaches `PASS` from reading or static composition alone when the gate requires rendered, accessibility, responsive, multilingual, device, or human-observation evidence.
