## Candidate A — compact operational grotesk hypothesis
Intended learning question: can a moderately compact sans maintain open counters and calm texture without collapsing joins at 14–17px?

Drawing hypotheses:
- H: relatively compact width; neutral verticals; crossbar near optical center, adjusted only after HHOH texture review.
- O: visibly round but not geometric-circle literalism; modest overshoot; inner counter slightly more regular than outer contour.
- n: compact shoulder with deliberately open inner join; avoid dark stem/shoulder knot.
- o: related to O but not mechanically scaled; preserve lowercase counter openness at x-height.

Primary failure risks: cramped H counter, dark n join, O/o becoming mechanically circular, small-size closure.

## Candidate B — open humanist-leaning hypothesis
Intended learning question: can wider/open counters improve internal clarity without producing loose operational texture or inconsistent stem/curve color?

Drawing hypotheses:
- H: slightly wider proportion than A with more breathing room around the crossbar.
- O: broader counter and less rigidly geometric tension; overshoot independently tuned.
- n: more generous shoulder aperture and smoother join transition.
- o: open internal shape with optical curve compensation rather than scaled O construction.

Primary failure risks: excessive width in operational strings, weak family compactness, uneven stem/curve color, shoulder softness.

## Falsification matrix
Both candidates must be rejected or revised if any of these appear before spacing work:
1. H/O cap texture cannot be made coherent without pair-specific spacing tricks;
2. O/o counters appear pinched or inflated relative to H/n white space;
3. n join creates a persistent dark spot at 14/17px;
4. overshoot produces obvious apparent height mismatch at target raster;
5. curve weight appears lighter/heavier than straight stems without intentional family logic;
6. A/B differ only numerically but not perceptually/structurally.

## Required proof board
For each A/B drawing artifact, capture in one board:
- enlarged black outline and reversed countershape view;
- baseline, x-height, cap-height and overshoot guides;
- `HHOH`, `OHO`, `nono`, `noon`, `HOno`;
- isolated glyphs at 14/17/24px;
- same strings with kerning OFF;
- critique labels limited to proportion, contour tension, countershape, weight compensation, join grammar, raster survival.

Sidebearings may be neutral construction values for the first drawing board; they are not optimized until a drawing survives critique. Kerning remains blocked.
