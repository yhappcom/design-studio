## Executed artifact
`T022-HOno-control-drawing-A-B.svg` contains explicit vector outlines for two hypotheses and a shared proof board. It was rendered in installed Chromium at 1200×900 and visually inspected. The board includes enlarged H/O/n/o and 14/17/24px em-height proxy rows. Kerning is absent; sidebearings are construction-only.

## Candidate A critique — compact operational grotesk
### KEEP
- A is materially more compact than B and therefore remains a distinct hypothesis rather than a numeric clone.
- O has explicit overshoot relative to H and its counter is not a simple scaled outer contour.
- The 14/17/24 proxy row preserves basic H/O/n/o identity.

### REVISE
- **n join/shoulder:** the shoulder entry produces an obvious diagonal/wedge discontinuity at enlarged size. This is a drawing defect, not spacing; A fails the current gate until the join is redrawn.
- **o counter:** the lowercase o counter is too rectangular/flat relative to its outer contour, producing a heavy mechanical ring rather than a coherent relation to O.
- **weight relation:** H verticals and O ring are not yet optically reconciled; the round feels heavier in the proof board.
- **texture:** H/O are very blunt/monoline while n/o introduce a different tension grammar. The four controls do not yet read as one mature system.

## Candidate B critique — wider/open hypothesis
### KEEP
- B creates a visibly wider H/O/n/o system and larger internal white areas, satisfying the requirement that A/B be structurally distinct.
- O and o widths clearly test the intended open-counter direction.

### REVISE
- **n join/shoulder:** the same wedge problem is more conspicuous in B; the attempted openness became a broken join rather than a controlled aperture.
- **o proportion:** the o is excessively wide and horizontally emphatic relative to n; this risks loose operational texture before spacing is even considered.
- **H/O relation:** H width expands more successfully than the lowercase system; cap and lowercase proportions do not yet share a convincing family logic.
- **curve color:** B's O/o round mass still appears heavier than H/n stems despite the more open counters.

## Gate decision
**DRAWING FAIL / REVISION REQUIRED for both A and B.** This is productive evidence: the first actual board falsifies the idea that the written hypotheses alone were sufficiently specified.

Do not tune sidebearings. Do not introduce kerning. The next action is a contour revision focused on:
1. reconstruct n as a continuous stem→shoulder→right-stem contour without the wedge;
2. make o inner/outer tension relational rather than rectangular-ring construction;
3. rebalance round/stem apparent weight;
4. rerender the same board before adding S/5 or any repertoire.

## Method lesson
T021 failed partly because engineering completeness could hide drawing weakness. T022 now catches defects at four-glyph scope. SVG/browser rendering is used as a reproducible critique carrier, not as proof of a production font or native rasterizer.
