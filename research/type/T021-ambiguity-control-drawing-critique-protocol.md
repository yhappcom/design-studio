## Drawing critique — kerning OFF
For each form record:
1. construction source and revision ID;
2. advance width and left/right sidebearings;
3. cap/x-height/baseline/overshoot relation as applicable;
4. stem/curve apparent-weight relation at 14/17/24px;
5. distinctive cue used for I/1/l or O/0;
6. whether that cue survives all three proof sizes without relying on color;
7. any contour defect visible before spacing judgment.

A cue is rejected if it improves one ambiguity pair by creating a new operational ambiguity or visibly breaks the family construction logic.

## Spacing critique

Classify failures as D drawing, S general spacing, R raster/size, F fallback/notdef. Do not create P pair-residual evidence until D/S/R/F are cleared across the bounded subset.
