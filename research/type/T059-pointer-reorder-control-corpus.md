# T059 — Pointer reorder control corpus under drawing gate

Status: Stage 2 PRACTICE / T021 DRAWING GATE UNCHANGED

## Purpose
Add future product-transfer strings created by the non-drag reorder requirement without allowing UI pressure to bypass Type's gate sequence.

Corpus families: Move up, Move down, Move to…, First, Previous, Next, Last, Undo move, position numerals 1/35…35/35, representative EN/KO object labels, DEP/ARR, PIC, SIC/FO, PICUS, SPIC, PF/PM, T/O/L/D.

## Gate discipline
T021 bounded drawing remains upstream. Widths/sidebearings stay frozen and kerning remains OFF until drawing acceptance permits general spacing and only then residual kerning. Long controls, Korean expansion, 200% wrapping or narrow rows are Layout/Content transfer pressure, not permission to narrow immature glyphs or repair drawing defects with tracking/kerning.

## Future transfer
After drawing/spacing gates, raster the same control corpus at representative product sizes and compare baseline, 200%, EN/KO and compact/expanded control presentations. Until then this document is corpus preparation, not typography closure evidence.
