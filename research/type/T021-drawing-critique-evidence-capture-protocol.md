## Capture unit
Every critique observation MUST bind:
- candidate A or B;
- source/build commit SHA and binary hash;
- kerning OFF assertion;
- specimen size 14/17/24px;
- proof string ID;
- glyph or glyph sequence;
- crop/coordinate reference or artifact path;
- defect class;
- severity;
- observation separated from interpretation;
- proposed drawing repair;
- rerender ID after repair.

## Required proof families
1. `I l 1` and mixed operational strings containing them.
2. `O 0` and mixed identifier/time strings.
3. H/O/n/o control skeleton for apparent weight and counter logic.
4. Straight/diagonal capitals including A/V/T/L where present.
5. digits 0–9 in time and totals.
6. punctuation `- : ,` in operational context.
7. É path and vertical-metric clipping check.

## Defect classes
Drawing defects are recorded before spacing: contour logic, curve tension, join/shoulder construction, aperture/counter construction, terminal policy, apparent stroke-weight inconsistency, ambiguity-critical differentiation, and size-specific raster collapse attributable to drawing. Sidebearing rhythm is deferred to general-spacing critique unless the observation proves a drawing cause.

## Decision rule
No A/B winner may be declared from metadata, token sensitivity, coverage or preference. A candidate advances only after high-severity drawing defects are repaired or explicitly bounded, with rerender evidence. Kerning remains OFF. Pair-specific fixes are prohibited in this gate.
