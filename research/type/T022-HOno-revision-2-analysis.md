## Construction decisions
1. Rebuild `n` as one continuous outer contour rather than a rectangular stem plus overlapping shoulder mass. The shoulder must enter and leave the stem without a visible wedge or doubled black region.
2. Rebuild `o` outer and counter curves as related but non-parallel optical contours. Counter tension must not be produced by simple uniform inset.
3. Compare vertical stem mass in `H/n` against top/bottom and side mass in `O/o`; numerical equality is not the target—apparent weight is.
4. Preserve A compact vs B open as genuinely different width hypotheses. Do not converge them merely to make critique easier.
5. Hold repertoire at H/O/n/o. `S/5`, spacing strings and kerning remain blocked until the four controls form a coherent grammar.
