Classification: **PRACTICE + METHOD CORRECTION + CRITIQUE CONTRACT**

## Purpose
Execute the first concrete step of the original-type-design mastery track without reopening the rejected T021 whole-family product route.

## Why H/O/n/o first
These four controls expose a large share of a sans family’s visual grammar before repertoire breadth hides defects:
- `H`: vertical stem weight, cap proportion, crossbar placement, straight counter rhythm;
- `O`: round width, overshoot, curve tension, inner/outer contour relation;
- `n`: lowercase stem/shoulder transition, x-height rhythm, join/tension;
- `o`: lowercase round width, overshoot and counter relation.

The purpose is not to make four attractive isolated symbols. A viable control set must establish repeatable relationships that later glyphs can inherit.

## Drawing gate
Before sidebearing or kerning work, a candidate must document and survive critique for:
1. cap height/x-height relationship;
2. H width-to-height and stem system;
3. O/o overshoot relative to flat H/n reference heights;
4. outer/inner curve tension and extrema placement;
5. countershape continuity rather than contour-only beauty;
6. n shoulder entry/exit and join darkness;
7. apparent stem/curve weight compensation;
8. black/white balance at enlarged outline and 14/17/24px raster.

No universal numeric proportion is predeclared as correct. Values become family hypotheses and are judged relationally.

## Required artifact sequence
For each materially different candidate A/B:
`construction note → enlarged outline proof → black/white counter proof → HHOH / OHO / nono / noon strings → 14/17/24 raster → critique → revised drawing`.

Only after revised drawing PASS may general sidebearings be tuned. Kerning remains blocked until base drawing and spacing stabilize.

## Explicit T021 method correction
T021 demonstrated that deterministic token operation and complete bounded coverage can coexist with weak drawing. Therefore automation in T022 Track B is limited initially to reproduction, measurement, rasterization and comparison. It must not become the source of unexamined optical decisions.

## Critique vocabulary
Record defects as one of:
- proportion;
- contour tension;
- countershape;
- weight compensation;
- join/terminal grammar;
- raster survival;
- general spacing;
- pair-specific residual.

The final category is the only one eligible for later kerning evidence.

## Transfer boundary
This exercise is curriculum evidence. It becomes a LogMate product hypothesis only if a later bounded family survives drawing/spacing gates and demonstrates a concrete role advantage over the mature shipped baseline.
