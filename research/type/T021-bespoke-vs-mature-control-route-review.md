# T021 — Bespoke route vs mature product-control review

Classification: **METHOD COMPARISON + ROUTE DECISION**

## Question
After normalized A/B architecture reached executable 11/11 but both candidates failed drawing, what evidence would justify continued bespoke authorship for LogMate?

## RELATED DOMAIN CHECK
- Color: operational identity cannot rely on hue.
- Layout/Interaction: current Stage 3 work needs flexible metrics across three surfaces, not frozen bespoke widths.
- Web: W021 now uses mature current-product/system typography while custom transfer is blocked.
- Content: identifiers, figures and localization strings cannot be shortened to accommodate a font experiment.

## Comparison dimensions
| Dimension | Current bespoke A/B evidence | Mature product-control route |
|---|---|---|
| bounded operational coverage | 36/36 PASS | expected product baseline; exact same-corpus proof still required |
| architecture intentionality | explicit 11/11 semantic architecture PASS | not authored by studio; mature external family design |

| identity value | unproven | neutral/control; not a claim of brand distinctiveness |

## Route criterion
Continued bespoke authorship is justified only if a custom route demonstrates a **specific product advantage** under the same operational corpus that cannot be obtained more cheaply/reliably through role-specific use of mature typography. Candidate advantages could include a materially better operational ambiguity system or a distinctive display identity, but neither is currently proven.

## Narrow-role alternative
The comparison introduces a third route rather than a false binary:
1. **full bespoke UI/text family** — currently unsupported;
2. **mature product typography for operational/body UI + narrow bespoke identity/display role** — viable hypothesis if identity value can be demonstrated without carrying dense operational text;
3. **defer bespoke entirely** — retain mature typography until a concrete identity requirement emerges.

Because the current custom failure is concentrated in text-family coherence and target-size operational texture, a narrow display role is methodologically more plausible than forcing the same unfinished family into dense logbook data. This is a route hypothesis, not a product recommendation or human-preference result.

## Decision
**STOP full-family bespoke repair as the default next activity.** The next exact Type proof should use the same bounded corpus with the actual current LogMate proportional Roboto artifact/source if retrievable, then compare raster/metrics and ambiguity sets. Until that evidence exists, no claim that Roboto is superior is made.

Spacing/T022 remains closed for the bespoke candidates. If a narrow identity/display route is later pursued, it needs a new role-specific drawing brief rather than reopening the failed operational-family brief unchanged.

## HANDOFFS TO OTHER SPECIALISTS
- Layout/Web: keep mature/flexible typography as the transfer control.
- Content: do not modify operational strings for typography fit.
- Color: do not add hue as an identifier disambiguation mechanism.

## Evidence boundary
This review compares routes and evidence burden. It does not provide human recognition/preference evidence, exact Roboto same-corpus raster evidence, or a final product typography selection.
