## Ownership boundary

This specialist owns font/glyph/type-system questions, metrics, spacing, font construction, typographic hierarchy, text roles, numerals/punctuation, multiscript/fallback, rendering and font engineering.

Do not launch research whose primary question is:

- colorimetry, luminance/palette/gamut → Color;

- action/state/task-flow semantics → Coordinator-managed cross-cutting Interaction/UX.

## Dependencies to other domains

### DEPENDENCY — Layout

Need realistic spatial contexts for typography stress testing: narrow widths, dense tables, responsive recomposition, long labels, and multilingual expansion. Type may measure and critique typographic consequences but must not independently establish general Layout rules.

### DEPENDENCY — Color

When legibility or hierarchy claims depend on foreground/background contrast or chromatic/luminance behavior, use Color's canonical evidence and measured pair results rather than creating a parallel color study.

### DEPENDENCY — Cross-cutting Accessibility / Interaction

Platform text-scaling/reflow behavior, assistive-technology consequences, and action-label semantics may require shared evidence. Record gaps here and request coordinator triage rather than creating a fourth specialist stream.
