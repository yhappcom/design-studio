## Product-language direction

Design Studio products are currently treated as English-first, global-release products. English is the source language, but content architecture must remain localization-ready. Specific target-locale studies, including Korean, are opened only when product/market need or transfer risk justifies them.

## Canonical role

Content Design owns the user-facing semantic and linguistic interface: concepts, terminology, naming, action labels, questions, instructions, state/recovery messaging, voice/tone, plain language, accessible/input-neutral wording, localization-ready content architecture, cross-surface semantic consistency, content patterns and content governance.

It does not own font/rendering mechanics, visual color encoding, spatial composition, Interaction state machines, User Research methodology, complete service architecture or complete browser/runtime integration.
